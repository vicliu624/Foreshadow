# Foreshadow brand usage guidelines

English · [简体中文](BRANDING.zh-CN.md)

These guidelines explain how to identify Foreshadow and distinguish upstream work
from independent distributions, forks, plugins and services. They cover the
Foreshadow name as used for this project and any project-owned logos, only to the
extent the relevant rightsholders control those rights. They do not assert
trademark registration, worldwide exclusivity over the word "Foreshadow", or
ownership of third-party marks or artwork.

## GPL rights take precedence for the software

The application and first-party work within [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) remain
**GPL-3.0-or-later**. These guidelines are not an EULA, an additional software
license, or a condition requiring acceptance before running, modifying or
redistributing that work. The GPL text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) remains unmodified.

In particular, these guidelines add no noncommercial condition, resale ban,
approval requirement, mandatory donation, requirement to use official signatures,
or prohibition on replacing libraries. They do not require every modified version
to change its name. GPL-required attribution and modification notices continue to
apply; no particular logo, screen layout, compatibility identifier or advertising
placement is made a condition of the software copyright grant.

GPL section 7 distinguishes copyright permission from misrepresentation of origin
and trademark rights. These guidelines address accurate identification and any
brand permission the project can give. They do not expand copyright restrictions
or limit uses independently permitted by applicable law.

## Uses allowed without asking

To the extent the project controls the relevant brand rights, the following
accurate, non-misleading uses are permitted without a separate approval or royalty.
The same permissions apply to commercial and noncommercial activity.

| Use | Example and appropriate identification |
|---|---|
| Discussion, documentation, reviews and comparison | "A tutorial for Foreshadow"; factual reference to the project does not imply endorsement. |
| Redistribution of an upstream version, including paid distribution | "Foreshadow 0.2.12 — redistributed by Acme"; identify the independent distributor and follow the applicable software licenses. |
| Independently built or modified versions and forks | "Foreshadow — community fork by Acme" or "Acme Analyzer, based on Foreshadow"; reasonably identify the publisher and build/modification status so the version is not mistaken for an official upstream release. Retaining the Foreshadow name is allowed with that clear distinction. |
| Independent compatible plugins and toolboxes | "Acme receiver plugin for Foreshadow"; describe compatibility accurately and identify the plugin's publisher. Compatibility wording is not a claim of official certification. |
| Independent support, training and other services | "Independent support for Foreshadow by Acme"; accurately identify the service provider and any actual relationship with the project. |
| Screenshots, source attribution and project-owned logos | Use them to identify or illustrate the project accurately. Copying or changing a GPL-covered asset remains subject to its copyright license; retaining it in a clearly identified distribution does not require a separate brand approval under these guidelines. |

These are examples, not a closed list of allowed factual references. The
permissions do not cover rights in third-party names, images or logos; their
owners' terms and applicable law continue to apply.

## Avoid misleading claims of upstream origin or endorsement

Do not falsely describe an independent build, modified program, plugin, service or
website as an official Foreshadow release, an upstream-maintained product, or an
officially certified or endorsed offering. Do not forge an upstream signature or
present your changes as having been published or approved by the maintainer.
An independent publisher should make its own identity reasonably clear in the
relevant distribution or service description. An accurate source attribution,
project name or compatibility identifier alone is not a claim of endorsement.

Different branding is one available way to avoid confusion, not a blanket
requirement. You may remove or adapt GPL-covered branding as the GPL permits,
while preserving required legal notices. Compatibility command names, settings
roots, package IDs and file formats need not be renamed merely to distinguish
a fork. No change to those technical protocols is required by this document.

For use beyond these permissions that would represent an actual upstream
partnership, certification or endorsement, obtain the relevant maintainer's
permission before making that representation. These guidelines do not supply
such an endorsement themselves. Exercising GPL rights or making an allowed
factual reference does not require contacting the project.
